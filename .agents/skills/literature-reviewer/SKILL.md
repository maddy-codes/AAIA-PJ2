# Literature Reviewer Instructions

## Overview
This skill specializes in the systematic review of academic literature for the Advanced AI Applications module. It focuses on identifying recent advancements in Reinforcement Learning (RL) and Natural Language Processing (NLP).

## Workflows
1. **Paper Discovery**: Search for papers related to the baseline method (Narasimhan et al. 2015) and recent extensions.
2. **Critical Analysis**: Evaluate the methodology, results, and limitations of selected papers.
3. **Synthesis**: Compare multiple papers and identify research gaps or potential improvements for the current project.
4. **Referencing**: Ensure all citations follow the Harvard referencing style.
